# Sweep30: a supported chest/pelvis rig extension

Sweep30 adds separately editable chest and pelvis headings to a new copy of
Sweep25. A broad torso field and gray-leg accommodation connect the turning
chest to planted paws. The seven-pose native pilot and complete continuous
frame review pass with limitations. The portable comparison includes the
unchanged source, five native videos and an editable native download.
This is a candidate, not owner adoption or a full motion-release certificate.

## Result and controlled comparison

The four-second action has one preparation, inward work gesture and relaxed
return. Its event roles follow Sweep29's independent front original. Angles
and in-betweens are hand-authored, not estimated joints or video-coordinate
fitting. The previous arm shape, material length and shoulder/elbow action are
retained deliberately to isolate torso responsibility.

A and B share the exact chest, shoulder, arm, hand, head and broom tasks.
A rotates the body/thigh branch as one rigid frame. B turns the chest above a
quieter pelvis and fades gray lower attachment motion near the fixed paws.
The tail follows the lower frame. The comparison also isolates relative turn
and leg anchoring by disabling each at the work-end pose.

Across all seven keys and four views, B reduces lower-body rotation without
changing the arm task. The belly pigment follows the broad torso twist. The
front and oblique views show the changed relationship between the chest and
supported legs; top view primarily shows head and tail directions because
the large head hides the torso. The sixteen isolated shaded views show no
observed new abrupt waist pinching, fold, crack or exposed attachment gap in
this pilot. Existing simplified gray-leg/paw overlap and arm-root embedding
remain visible limitations. Bright clay highlights limit subtle shading
judgment; oblique/side surfaces were also inspected at their native640px.

The native is intentionally more restrained than the generated source's full
broom excursion. This experiment establishes useful separate rig controls;
it does not claim that lower-body articulation alone reproduces every part of
the source performance. The generated tail silhouette is not adopted as new
anatomy or pigment authority.

## Qualification and scope

`qualification-ledger.json` tracks all15historical architecture issues and
all11gate groups. All remain open at full-release scope. The receipts above
support a bounded, useful sweep-rig experiment; they do not close jump
phases, airborne foot shape, general asymmetric/alternating support,
all-control collision/strain ranges, force balance or target runtime export.

No independent reviewer or external model was used. Geometry checks and
main-agent visual findings are separate. Existing adopted motion registries,
Sweep25 and the original reference videos remain unchanged. Read
RIG_GUIDE.md for the persistent controls and what requires revalidation.
