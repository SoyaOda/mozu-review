# Constructive extension and its limits

The original model owns the animated surfaces. Sweep36 removes the Sweep33
height-weighted post-composition warp. Its common placement is one rotation R
and translation d about the material reference P=(0,0.015,0.32):

`x_body = P + R (x_rest - P) + d`.

This preserves every Body source distance, angle and profile. The same transform
carries both hip task points, shoulders, local analytic arms, neck attachment and
tail. The currentG paws and their two contact frames remain unchanged. The pivot
is an authoring reference; it is not a measured center of pressure or mass.

## Independent thighs

Each original180 profile receives its own transformed hip and original fixed
ankle. The native positive affine thigh task changes the upper ellipsoid's
axis/length while keeping its transverse scales at1. An invertible affine map
preserves that upper ellipsoid's convexity. No remote sole rotates successive
material slices, and no final mesh correction follows the construction.

The retained198 lower family uses the original upper section E, fixed currentG
endpoint B, upper center A and tangent T. Its center and radius are:

`C(t)=A+tT+t^2(B-A-T)`, `r(t)=sqrt(1-(1-width^2)t^2)`, `width=0.84`.

Thus every section remains a positive homothetic ellipse. The radius is monotone
and concave, with no new radial peaks or local waves. The centerline is quadratic
and matches the original upper tangent and fixed lower endpoint. The original
tangent-matched concealed closure remains inside the separate paw and requires
actual containment verification after the task change.

With the retained vertical paw frame, `T_z=B_z-A_z`, so `C_z(t)=A_z+t(B_z-A_z)`.
When the upper ring remains above the fixed endpoint, height is strictly
monotone through the open loft. Positive horizontal ellipse sections at distinct
heights cannot cross one another. This construction property concerns the open
taper; it does not certify the cap, upper junction or final hip union, which
still require the recorded native surface checks.

The all-direction centroid acceleration gauge is
`sqrt(C''xy^T E^-1 C''xy)`. It measures bending against the actual section sizes,
not vertex strain. On all three current161-time pilot paths it is no greater than
the neutral value0.566782. This is a conservative comparison of centerline
curvature, not a global silhouette certificate. In particular, the adopted
neutral taper already fails the sufficient all-direction convexity condition
`gauge <= 1-width^2` (0.56678 versus0.2944). Sweep36 does not falsely impose or claim
global convexity on this original lower attachment or on the groin.
