are interpreted by each part's role: forward balance during descent, release
after sitting, then quiet food support. Independent generated views are not
synchronized cameras of one measured action. No image-coordinate fitting,
numerical pose search or source-curve reconstruction is used in this revision.

radius profile and rounded hand. A body-surface shoulder attachment, broad elbow,
independent wrist and axial hand roll separate the controls by anatomical role.

shoulder before lowering past the thigh. Relaxed rest matches the standing arm
after translation with the shoulder. The final approach joins the existing longer
food-hold task; the original evaluated arm takes over exactly from frame178.

The original r12, prior E7, original references and adopted releases are preserved.
