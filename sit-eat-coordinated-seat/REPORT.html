## Root cause and implementation

The previous bodyY control moved only the tail; the body, shoulders, thighs,
paws and arms did not follow it. A root-object Y translation also had no effect
on those visible parts. The source fit had represented the missing body retreat
as excess foot movement. Retiming that compensation alone would preserve the
underlying ownership defect.

A copied C164 body map now carries one shared Y translation for the body,
shoulder carrier and material hip points. The rigid neck carrier and tail root
receive the same offset. The existing thigh profile bridges the transported hip
and independently controlled paw. Arms retain their existing shoulder binding;
the food follows the hands. This introduces no new image-coordinate optimizer,
script handler or driver namespace. The original group remains in the file.

The authored retreat distance is 0.52 native units, selected from two reviewed
pilots. Standing begins forward of the preserved seated endpoint. Paw support
holds through frame 25; unfolding, pitch and spread finish together at 67. The
new adapter is inactive from 177. Its zero-offset geometry bypass is exact.
