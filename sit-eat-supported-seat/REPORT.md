## Confirmed causes and repair

The prior C179 thigh map had an unbounded longitudinal scale derived from actual
hip-to-ankle reach. Its scale rose from1.0 at rest to1.6652 at frame67. This was
actual native deformation, not only a camera effect. Same-index scaffold edge
ratios are supporting diagnostics, not separately verified physical strain.

The previous paw target was too far from the seated hip, and paw opening
continued as the pelvis approached its endpoint. Timing alone left the1.6652
ratio. A reach guard alone bounded length but moved the feet during descent.

The new native adapter applies one floor-preserving XY reach correction to the
paw, rigid frame, ankle collar and thigh. A smooth ceiling bounds ratio at1.04
when vertical reach is feasible. Impossible vertical reach is explicitly tested.
The chosen motion requests a closer paw position and smaller standing retreat.
Feet finish at frame49 (0.80s); coupled pelvis retreat/descent ends at73 (1.20s).
The selected final ratio is approximately1.0016, so the default movement needs
only negligible reach correction. It does not depend on a large reactive slide.

The first saved prototype was rejected by the independent dense oracle: it
mirrored the accepted left support origin, but the actual right origin differs
slightly. The final r03 adapter reads each side's ankle/support constants from
the inherited native graph. A captured-data regression reproduces the unwanted
standing correction; the saved candidate must meet the original numerical
limits. The failed r01 native, source hashes and audit remain preserved.

The next dense trial found a subframe support gap: the accurate inherited hull
was clamped to upward correction only. The final adapter uses signed correction
for supported seating and feeds the common frame before reach resolution.
A disposable dense trial proves that disabling this correction restores r02
and enabling it closes the gap without changing paw shape or thresholds.
Failed r02 evidence is also retained.
