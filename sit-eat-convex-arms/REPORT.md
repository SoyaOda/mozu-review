# Rounded eating arms — r03

## What changed

The eating arm now stays in one convex, rounded shape family throughout the complete meal. An analytic attachment places the whole proximal cap below the unchanged shoulder guide and inside the body, while the original material food-grip vertex remains exact. The body, face, feet, food and inherited action keys are preserved.

## Why the earlier claim was insufficient

Arm124/126 guaranteed local sweep regularity; it did not prove every projected outline natural. The articulated extension and later fair-arm layer could still create a notch or swelling. The first two convex prototypes passed shape checks but failed assembled shoulder appearance. They were rejected before a full render. Those natives and their failed reviews remain preserved.

## Mathematical construction

The template is the convex hull of two balls, using the original size parameters plus an internal proximal seat. A single nonsingular affine map preserves convexity. A closed-form body-up support equation sets the shoulder ceiling. Each of 73728 unchanged body facets gives an analytic inward-entry bound; a C1 upper positive-part and L32 aggregate conservatively contain the cap. There is no image objective, camera-dependent shape, per-frame outline table, iterative optimizer or local elbow patch. The cone/cap join is tangent-continuous (C1), not curvature-continuous (C2).
